PCOD & PCOS Reversal: An Ayurvedic Path to Hormonal Harmony

One in five Indian women lives with PCOD or PCOS. The standard prescription — hormonal pills — quiets the symptoms but leaves the underlying imbalance untouched. Ayurveda asks a different question: what disturbed your dosha balance in the first place?

Vaidya Dolly Solanki Makwana, MD (Panchakarma) Apr 14, 2026 8 min read

If you have been told you have PCOD or PCOS, you have probably also been told some version of: "There is no cure. Take this pill. Lose some weight. Come back when you want to conceive." That is the standard script — and for many women, it does not feel like a real answer.

From the Ayurvedic point of view, PCOD and PCOS are not a single disease but a syndrome — metabolic dysfunction, hormonal imbalance, chronic inflammation, and disturbed reproductive channels all tangled together. The exact pattern differs from woman to woman, which is why a single drug rarely fixes it. Treatment must follow the pattern.

The Ayurvedic Diagnosis

In classical texts, the symptoms of PCOD/PCOS map most closely to Aartava-Kshaya (depletion of menstrual tissue) and Granthi (cystic formation), driven by:

  • Kapha excess — sluggish metabolism, weight gain, oily skin, heaviness
  • Medo-dushti — disturbed fat metabolism leading to insulin resistance
  • Apana Vata vitiation — disturbed downward-moving energy that governs menstruation
  • Pitta-Pradoshaja Vyadhi — heat and inflammation as a secondary layer

This explains why women with PCOS are not all the same. A heavyset woman with sluggish digestion needs a very different protocol from a thin, anxious woman with irregular cycles and no weight gain. The label is the same; the treatment is not.

The Aksharam PCOD/PCOS Reversal Protocol

Our reversal program runs over three to six months and combines:

The Five Pillars

  • Panchakarma cleanse — typically Virechana and Uttara Basti to clear Ama, reset the uterine environment, and restore Apana Vata
  • Targeted herbs — Shatavari, Ashoka, Lodhra, Kanchanara Guggulu, Triphala — chosen based on your dominant pattern
  • Customised diet plan — Kapha-pacifying, low-glycaemic, anti-inflammatory, dosha-aware
  • Movement protocol — yoga sequences specifically for the pelvic region; Surya Namaskara if Kapha-dominant
  • Lifestyle redesign — sleep timing, stress management, screen-light hygiene

What to Expect Realistically

This is not a quick fix. Most women see meaningful changes by month two — clearer skin, more regular cycles, steady energy. By month four to six, the typical results are: cycles within 28–32 days, ovulation restored, insulin sensitivity improved, weight stabilised, mood lifted. Many women have conceived naturally within a year of completing the program.

What is happening on the inside is the body finally rebuilding rather than suppressing. Inflammation drops. The ovary stops being a passive cyst-storage and starts ovulating again. The hormonal axis re-synchronises with the lunar rhythm it was meant to follow. The shift is gradual but durable — because it addresses cause, not symptom.

What Aksharam Will Not Do

We will not promise that you can stop your hormonal medication overnight. Many women come to us already on metformin, hormonal pills, or insulin. Tapering off these is done carefully, with your gynaecologist in the loop, only when the underlying physiology has actually shifted. We work alongside modern medicine, not against it.
