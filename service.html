Our Treatment Menu

A comprehensive range of classical Ayurvedic therapies, each precisely indicated for specific health conditions and applied with expert skill.

🔥

Kati Basti

Pooling of warm medicated oil in a dough ring over the lower back to relieve lumbar pain, sciatica, disc herniation, and degenerative spine conditions.

💧

Janu Basti

Warm medicated oil retention over the knee joint — highly effective for osteoarthritis, ligament injuries, and knee pain without invasive procedures.

🌿

Upanaha Sweda

Herbal paste poultice applied to affected joints and muscles, producing deep therapeutic sweat that relieves stiffness, swelling, and local pain effectively.

🌾

Lepana

Application of medicated herbal paste over the body or specific areas to treat skin diseases, inflammation, oedema, and joint stiffness through transdermal absorption.

🫧

Pichu

Continuous application of medicated oil-soaked cotton pad on the spine or head — ideal for cervical spondylosis, headaches, and cranial nerve disorders.

🔆

Agnikarma

Ancient thermal micro-cauterisation therapy using heated metallic probes to treat chronic musculoskeletal pain, plantar fasciitis, and neuropathic conditions.

🛁

Avagaha

Medicated herbal sitz bath in which the patient is immersed in a warm decoction — effective for haemorrhoids, fistula, pelvic disorders, and skin conditions.

🧠

Shiro Abhyanga

Classical medicated head massage stimulating marma points to relieve stress, migraine, hair loss, insomnia, and tension headaches with deep relaxation.
