Our Treatment Menu

A comprehensive range of classical Ayurvedic therapies, each precisely indicated for specific health conditions and applied with expert skill.

Kati Basti

Warm medicated oil pooled in a dough ring over the lower back to relieve lumbar pain, sciatica, and disc herniation.

Janu Basti

Warm medicated oil retention over the knee joint — effective for osteoarthritis, ligament injuries, and knee pain.

Upanah Sweda

Herbal paste poultice applied to affected joints producing deep therapeutic sweat that relieves stiffness and swelling.

Lepana

Application of medicated herbal paste over the body to treat skin diseases, inflammation, oedema, and joint stiffness.

Pichu

Continuous application of medicated oil-soaked cotton pad on the spine or head — ideal for cervical spondylosis and headaches.

Agnikarma

Ancient thermal micro-cauterisation to treat chronic musculoskeletal pain, plantar fasciitis, and neuropathic conditions.

Avagaha

Medicated herbal sitz bath — effective for haemorrhoids, fistula, pelvic disorders, and inflammatory skin conditions.

Shiro Abhyanga

Classical medicated head massage stimulating marma points to relieve stress, migraine, hair loss, and insomnia.
